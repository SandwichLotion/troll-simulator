How to play

You are the creator on a LIVE. Guests join, some are great, and some are trolls waiting for the right moment. Keep the LIVE clean until you’re out of levels, or until TikTok ends it.

🎥Go play→

The goal

Earn as many 💎 diamonds as you can. You earn them by removing trolls and thanking good guests. You lose them, and your community health, when you act on someone who was innocent. If health reaches zero, TikTok ends your LIVE. Your best scores are saved on this device for each difficulty.

Your guests

  • Followers of @thetrollsimulator wear a Follower badge. They are always good guests. Follow the account on TikTok to be added in the next update.
  • Good guests say friendly things, send you Roses (+2 💎), and leave after a while unless you thank them.
  • Sleepers are trolls that act nice for a few minutes, then start yelling censored obscenities such as #@$%&!.
  • Link sharers bring friends. Watch for names tagged “via @someone” and the 🔗 on a box.
  • Raid groups are trolls who arrive together and wait until every member is seated. Then they all yell at once.

Your tools

Tap a guest’s box to open their profile card. It shows account age, followers, reports, who they joined through, and whether they shared the link, plus their TikTok bio if one was added in Admin. Tap the 🎙️ on a box to mute in one tap.

Removing or blocking a raid member before the group fires cancels their invites. That’s a bonus of +30 💎 for each raid you prevent.

Muted guests can come back. After a few seconds a muted guest may turn their mic back on. Trolls who return start yelling again, so remove or block repeat offenders.

Community health

The bar under the header is your community health. Every guest who is yelling drains it. It slowly recovers while the LIVE is quiet. At 0%, TikTok ends your LIVE for guideline violations.

Difficulty and comfort options

  • Easy, Medium, Hard change how fast guests join, how long sleepers stay quiet, and how fast health drains. Pick one before you go LIVE. Scores are kept separately for each.
  • Relaxed mode (in Settings) slows joins and health loss, and keeps good guests from leaving.
  • Pause (the ❚❚ button) freezes the LIVE. The game also pauses while a window is open.
  • Layouts (the ▦ button) switch between a grid, large boxes and a list.
  • Muted guests turning their mic back on, game sounds and soundbytes can each be switched off in Settings.

Keyboard

Press Tab to move between guest boxes. Press Enter or Space on a box to open the profile, or on its mic button to mute. Esc closes any window.

Customize the game

The Admin page is where you add your own guests and sounds:

  • Add TikTok usernames, with an optional bio, to the guest list. They always appear as friendly guests.
  • Upload a username sheet (username,type,bio) for custom good and bad guests.
  • Link mp3 soundbytes to the lines guests say, using a CSV (difficulty,Text They Say,mp3file) and a drag-and-drop grid.
  • Set clip size limits with a config.txt file.
  • Back up or restore your scores and settings.

Sharing

When a LIVE ends you can share your score to TikTok or save a score card image. Open ⋯ → Account to sign in with TikTok when it’s set up.
